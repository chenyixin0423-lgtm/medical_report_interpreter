## Scope and checking record

The test-description and result-interpretation sections were read for each page. The summaries retain only general possible associations, avoid asserting a patient's cause, and omit diagnostic cutoffs, treatment targets, medication changes and visit deadlines. Null directions mean no adequate directional summary was admitted, not that the original website has no discussion anywhere. Within-range explanations provide general context without declaring health.

- Blood counts: blood/whole-blood counts only, not urinary cells or platelet function.
- Chemistry/lipids: blood, serum or plasma only. Urine results are not covered. BUN is restricted to mg/dL of nitrogen; urea is not aliased to BUN.
- Glucose: fasting/nonfasting/unknown branches are caveats, not diagnostic criteria. No fasting duration is inferred.
- HbA1c: percentage only; red-cell/organ-condition limitations retained; no gestational-diabetes interpretation or personal target.
- Lipids: no individualized cardiovascular risk, calculated LDL, severity staging or treatment decisions. High HDL is not extrapolated to unlimited protection.
- The pages cover general education; the app does not establish applicability of pediatric, pregnancy-specific or disease-management targets. Printed interval confirmation remains necessary.

Initial ALT/AST long-form guessed URLs did not resolve; the actual `alt-blood-test/` and `ast-test/` pages were found and inspected instead. No inaccessible page was marked checked. All 16 accepted files are pinned in `curated/manifest.json`; old text files remain unchanged and unverified. The pin protects against accidental unchecked edits, not deliberate local tampering. Runtime does not crawl pages or claim continuous source freshness.
