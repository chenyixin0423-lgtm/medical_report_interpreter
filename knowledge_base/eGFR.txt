Indicator: eGFR (Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate)
Full name: Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate

Reference range: ≥ 90 mL/min/1.73 m²
Unit: mL/min/1.73 m²

Clinical significance:
eGFR is calculated from serum creatinine together with age, sex, and (in some equations) race. It estimates how much blood the kidneys filter per minute — the higher, the better. eGFR is the authoritative metric for staging chronic kidney disease (CKD) and is far more informative than creatinine alone.

CKD staging by eGFR:
| Stage | eGFR (mL/min/1.73 m²) | Description                        | Action                             |
|-------|------------------------|------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| G1    | ≥ 90                   | Normal or mildly reduced           | Monitor if proteinuria present     |
| G2    | 60 – 89                | Mildly reduced                     | Identify and address cause         |
| G3a   | 45 – 59                | Mild-to-moderate reduction         | Nephrology referral may be needed  |
| G3b   | 30 – 44                | Moderate-to-severe reduction       | Nephrology referral recommended    |
| G4    | 15 – 29                | Severely reduced                   | Prepare for renal replacement      |
| G5    | < 15                   | Kidney failure                     | Dialysis or transplant evaluation  |

Causes of reduced eGFR:
- Diabetic nephropathy (leading cause of CKD globally)
- Hypertensive nephrosclerosis
- Chronic glomerulonephritis
- Polycystic kidney disease
- Recurrent kidney infections or obstruction
- Physiological decline with ageing (≈ 5–10 mL/min per decade after age 40)

Notes:
- Adults > 60 years with eGFR 60–89 mL/min and no other evidence of kidney damage (normal urine, no proteinuria) may simply reflect normal age-related decline — not CKD.
- CKD is defined as kidney damage OR eGFR < 60 mL/min/1.73 m² persisting for > 3 months.
- An eGFR alone does not detect early kidney damage — always add a urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (uACR) for the most sensitive screening.
- eGFR < 30 (Stage G4): begin planning for renal replacement therapy (dialysis or transplantation).
- Acute changes in eGFR (over days to weeks) indicate acute kidney injury (AKI), not CKD — require urgent evaluation.
